# Cold history debt trend budget

## Evidence and objective

The postwarm 2026-09-30 23:31:52–23:37:53 UTC same-process observation is saved at `build/benchmarks/20261001-postwarm-bottleneck/http/README.md`. Cold publication was 5.27 blocks/s against 14.17 eligible blocks/s. The controller entered `debt_growth` six times and sampled level 1 four times, although Pebble estimated compaction debt fell from 7.59 to 2.64 GB over the whole window. L0 sublevels remained below threshold; device pressure, new stall, hard-limit reason entries, and write delay were zero. This does not prove every level-1 interval unnecessary, but two adjacent debt increases are insufficient evidence of persistent growth during compaction sawtooth.

Replace the debt-growth soft-pressure predicate and its debt-recovery evidence with bounded trend observations. Preserve immediate hard/write-stall, fresh device-pressure, and L0 protections; unavailable or stale engine samples stay unknown, and unavailable device samples still cap the maximum budget and disable CPU burst. Do not change DB, consensus, archive format, pruning, batch or recovery limits.

## Controller contract

Retain at most seven independent accepted engine debt observations. A sample must meet the existing freshness and five-second acceptance rules. Clear the trend on unavailable/stale/future engine observations, timestamp or stall-counter reset, or an interval exceeding 90 seconds between accepted samples. A reused sample cannot increase the point count or span. Device freshness is independent: unknown device data does not erase valid engine debt evidence, and existing device admission remains conservative.

First choose the newest geometrically mature suffix: at least three accepted points spanning at least 30 seconds. Its net direction determines whether recent debt is still rising. Only when that net is positive and current debt is at least 2 GiB may the controller extend the suffix backwards within the seven-point bound to find two independent positive adjacent steps and a positive extended net. This prevents a distant high point from concealing fresh sustained growth after a compaction drop, while an old pair of rises cannot keep pressure active after recent debt plateaus. Small intermediate retracements are allowed. Two local rises inside a falling 30-second window do not alone trigger level 1. A steady five-second observer can establish pressure after 30 seconds; a one-minute observer can establish it after three points spanning two minutes. Unknown and sparse observations cannot borrow elapsed time across a gap.

For recovery after any pressure, keep two independent good observations before leaving level 1 and three before reaching level 3. A mature *recent* suffix with nonpositive net debt can count as low debt even when its newest step is an upward sawtooth, provided L0 remains below the existing low threshold and no immediate protection applies. This prevents an overall falling debt sequence from indefinitely resetting recovery to zero. A newly growing recent suffix cancels that recovery even if an older retained point remains higher. The existing bounded batch sizes, duty targets, CPU-burst predicate, and recovery accounting are unchanged.

Expose `debt_trend_{points,span_ns,net_bytes,positive_steps}` for the recent geometrically mature suffix (or the available prefix before maturity), and `debt_trend_growth_{points,span_ns,net_bytes,positive_steps}` for the extended suffix only while it establishes pressure. Keep the existing consecutive-rise diagnostic. Reason bits and entry counters keep their existing meaning and bit assignments; `debt_growth` now records entry into sustained trend pressure.

## Verification and limits

Deterministic traces cover short and falling sawtooth, sustained monotonic rise at five-second and one-minute cadence, net rise with small retracements, a sparse old-high/new-growth sequence, a sparse growth-to-plateau release, hysteresis exit under falling sawtooth, renewed growth, repeated/invalid/reset/gapped samples, unknown device with independently growing engine debt, and independent device/L0 protection. Existing tests cover hard, stall, bounded batch sizing, recovery, and reason metrics. Seven points limit memory and per-update work, but an unusual sawtooth longer than the observation window can still trigger if its recent bounded trend is genuinely positive. Production impact requires a separate controlled same-process observation after deployment; this change is not deployed here.
